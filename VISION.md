# VISION: AI Real Estate Concierge ("Jules")

## Ultimate Goal
To build a high-performance "Agentic" Lead Conversion workflow that autonomously qualifies, nurtures, and converts real estate leads using a "Default Aggressive" strategy.

## Foundational Concepts
*   **State Machines over Linear Drips:** A lead's journey shouldn't be a generic drip campaign; it should be a reactive state machine that changes behavior based on the lead's intent, interaction, and urgency score.
*   **Warm Transfer First:** The system prioritizes getting qualified leads on a live call with a human agent within the first critical 5 minutes of engagement (The "Double Tap" and "10-Day Blitz" methods).
*   **Multi-Channel Dominance:** Touching leads relentlessly through Call, SMS, Email, Social Media, and Direct Mail until a definitive "Yes" or "No" is achieved.
*   **Decentralized Intelligence:** Utilizing Model Context Protocols (MCP) to allow the AI agent to query live inventory and contact databases in real-time.

## User-Satisfaction Design
*   Agents require a command-center view that instantly highlights "Who is hot?", "Who needs a call?", and "What is the next best action?". The UI must be fast, actionable, and distraction-free.
